Nordics Adabas & Natural User Group The Nordics Adabas & Natural User Group 2026 meeting takes place on Thursday, 26 November 2026, at the Tivoli Hotel & Congress Center in Copenhagen, Denmark. Hosted by Software AG, the event brings together the regional community to explore the future of mission-critical systems and discuss the long-term strategy for Adabas & Natural 2050+. The afternoon agenda features sessions on Natural Open API, zIIP benefits, resilience under DORA/NIS2 regulations, and updates to Natural and CONNX.
