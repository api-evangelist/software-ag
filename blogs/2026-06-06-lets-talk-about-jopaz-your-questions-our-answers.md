This community thread discusses JOPAZ, a mainframe optimization solution that compiles COBOL programs into Java bytecode to run on zIIP processors instead of central processors, reducing MSU consumption and software licensing costs without requiring application modifications. Community members noted JOPAZ is a smart way to squeeze more efficiency out of existing COBOL workloads without forcing a full rewrite, and the discussion covers the Proof of Value evaluation process for getting started.
